# R5 functional QA (iteration 5, lane B)

**TL;DR:** One serious break: **clicking any project card freezes the page for about 4.1 s in Chromium** (both viewports, 3 uncaught page errors per run) because the card-to-case View Transition waits on `requestAnimationFrame` while rendering is suppressed. The morph never plays. Reduced motion navigates in 160 to 570 ms. Everything else: 12 pages x 2 viewports have no console or network errors, no horizontal scroll, at most 1 canvas, no empty viewports and no third-party requests. Visitor picker, chip, modal, live stats, poll, walking guide (drop-in, scroll-follow, Walk with me, arrows, jump, Esc, hide and show), disclosures, say-my-name, Saigon desk, copy-email and GoCrypto walkthrough all work. Gaps: a returning legacy visitor cannot vote, ask-me chips ignore a role picked in the same tab, the guide's pills and bubble cover content at 390, depth-pill segments are 36 px tall on mobile, and the e2e gate is red from 2 stale specs. **Totals: 2 FAIL, 6 CONCERN, 5 OBSERVATION.**

## Gap list

Format: GAP-I5-F{n} | severity | evidence | file | fix

### FAIL

**GAP-I5-F1 | FAIL | card to case transition freezes the page ~4 s | `src/components/motion/transition-link.tsx:11-21,57-61`**
- Evidence (clean HEAD, both viewports): click a card on `/work`, home stack card, or the "next case" link. The first screenshot after the click takes **4126 ms (1440) and 4217 ms (390)**. Case URL and hero appear in the DOM at ~4.2 s. 3 uncaught page errors per run: `Transition was aborted because of timeout in DOM update. DOM update timed out`. A rAF sampler got 3 to 8 frames in 2.5 s. With reduced motion (plain navigation) the same click takes 163 to 568 ms with ~145 frames. Scroll also stays at the old position until the freeze ends (scrollY read 170, 3738 and 9672 at 1.5 s after arrival, 0 at 5 s).
- Cause: `waitForRoute()` polls with `requestAnimationFrame` inside the `startViewTransition` update callback. The browser suppresses rendering until that callback's promise settles, so rAF never fires and the UA's 4 s DOM-update timeout aborts the transition. The morph never runs. `vt.ready` rejection is also unhandled (only `vt.finished` is caught), which is the page error.
- Fix: poll with `setTimeout` or a `MutationObserver` (or await `router.push` then check `location.pathname`), keep a hard 1 to 1.5 s cap, `vt.ready.catch(() => undefined)` and `vt.updateCallbackDone.catch(...)`. Re-test: first screenshot after click under 400 ms and 0 page errors. Add an e2e that fails on a `pageerror` containing "DOM update timed out".

**GAP-I5-F2 | FAIL | repo gate: full e2e red at clean HEAD | `tests/e2e/case-studies.spec.ts:57`, `tests/e2e/site.spec.ts:176`**
- Evidence: full isolated run 189 pass / 5 fail. Re-run on clean export at low load: 18 pass / 2 fail, both deterministic.
  - `case-studies.spec.ts:57` gocrypto "interactive pieces respond": block 3 `PhoneWalkthrough` has buttons only in the `md:hidden` carousel, so at desktop nothing visible responds. Spec predates commit `9fb9e26`.
  - `site.spec.ts:176` "case-study hero is centred": clicks the first cosap bento `zoom-image`, which now sits in the Deep depth run (collapsed, `inert`, height 0 at the default Read depth, commit `b700bcd`). Times out after 120 s.
- Fix: gocrypto spec should drive the pinned scene by scrolling at desktop and the dots at mobile; the cosap test should `#deep` or click the depth stub first.

**GAP-I5-F5 | CONCERN | ask-me chips ignore a role chosen in the same tab | `src/components/site/ask-me.tsx` (listens to `storage` only)**

- Fix: read from `useVisitor()` (the store already holds the role) or dispatch a custom event on `setRole`.

**GAP-I5-F6 | CONCERN | walking guide covers content, mostly at 390 | `src/components/site/visitor/guide/guide.tsx` (`avoidRects` only protects the character box), `guide-controls.tsx`**
- Evidence (hit-test of guide rects against page elements, 6 scroll stops):
  - 390: pills ("Walk with me", "Hide character", 110x44 and 118x44, always visible) cover 23 percent of the Founder tile, 36 percent of Engineer, 24 to 26 percent of ticker items; at scrollY 3300 the passive bubble hides **73 percent of the "Ho Chi Minh City to Seoul / COSAP" row**; at drop-in the pills sit over the portrait's face; engaged state stacks bubble, key hint, 3 mini controls (44 px) and pills, covering the headline body text and roughly 45 percent of the viewport height.

- Fix: include the bubble and pill rects in `avoidRects`, flip the bubble to the side with fewer controls, make the pills a single 44 px icon button on touch (expand on engage), cap the engaged stack to bubble + controls on touch.
